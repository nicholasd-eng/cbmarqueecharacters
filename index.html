    Questions

    Marquee letter rental FAQ

    How much does it cost to rent marquee letters in Cape Breton?

    Marquee letters and numbers from CB Marquee Characters cost $50 per character plus HST, for up to 24 hours. Delivery, set-up and pick-up add $25 in the Sydney area. LOVE, for example, is four characters, so about $225 plus HST all in.

    What is included in a marquee letter rental?

    Every rental includes the characters you choose, delivery to your venue, set-up, and pick-up after the event, for up to 24 hours. You provide a regular outlet and a safe, flat spot. HST is extra and you get a written quote before anything is booked.

    How big are the marquee letters and numbers?

    Letters stand 3 feet tall and about 2 feet wide. Numbers stand 4 feet tall. All are painted white, fitted with warm bulbs, and need about 2 feet of width each.

    Can the marquee letters be set up outdoors?

    Yes. The letters work indoors or outdoors on a safe, flat surface with a regular outlet nearby. We have set them up in backyards, on the Port of Sydney boardwalk and at the Wentworth Park band shell.

    Where does CB Marquee Characters deliver?

    We are based in Sydney, Nova Scotia and deliver across the Cape Breton Regional Municipality, including Glace Bay, North Sydney, Sydney Mines, New Waterford, Membertou, Westmount and Coxheath. Delivery is $25 in the Sydney area. For Baddeck, Louisbourg, Port Hawkesbury and elsewhere on Cape Breton Island, tell us the venue and we will include delivery in your written quote.

    What can the letters spell?

    We rent letters, numbers and an ampersand, so a name, an age, a year or a word like LOVE or MR & MRS all work. Tell us what you want to spell and your date, and we will confirm every character is available. Popular dates go early, so it is worth asking as soon as you know.

    How do I book marquee letters?

    Call 902-537-1074, message us on Facebook or Instagram, or email admin@cbmarqueecharacters.ca with your word, your date and the venue. You will get a written quote before anything is booked, and you will be talking to the person who built the letters.
