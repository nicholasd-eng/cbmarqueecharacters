    Planning guide

    How to plan light-up letters for an event in Cape Breton

    Written by Nicholas Driscoll, owner of CB Marquee Characters. Updated October 2026.

    Most people book the letters two to six weeks before the event, and the planning takes about ten minutes. Here is the order we walk through on every quote, with the numbers you need.

    1. Pick the word and count the characters

      Every letter, number and ampersand counts as one character at $50. Spaces are free. LOVE is 4 characters, MR & MRS is 5, HAPPY BIRTHDAY is 13, and a two-digit age is 2. Send us the exact spelling and we will confirm every character is available for your date.

    2. Measure the space

      Letters stand 3 feet tall and need about 2 feet of width each, so LOVE takes roughly 8 feet of wall or lawn. Numbers stand 4 feet tall. Leave a foot of clearance on each side so people can stand beside the word for photos.

    3. Find the outlet

      The bulbs run from one regular household outlet, with no special wiring. If the outlet is more than a few metres away, tell us and we will bring an extension cord. One outlet is enough for a full word.

    4. Choose a flat, safe spot, indoors or out

      The letters sit on any flat surface: a banquet-hall stage, a backyard deck, the Port of Sydney boardwalk or the band shell at Wentworth Park have all worked. Outdoors, keep them off soft ground and away from standing water so the bases stay level.

    5. Think about the photo

      Place the word where the camera will point: behind the head table, beside the cake, at the entrance or at the dance floor edge. The letters read well lit and unlit, so daytime ceremonies and evening receptions both work. The warm bulbs photograph best once the room lights come down.

    6. Book the time window

      Each rental covers up to 24 hours. We deliver and set up before guests arrive, usually the morning of the event, and pick up after it ends or the next morning. Delivery, set-up and pick-up are included for $25 in the Sydney area, and quoted for the rest of Cape Breton Island.

    7. Confirm the budget

      LOVE is $225 plus HST. MR & MRS is $275 plus HST. A two-digit birthday age is $125 plus HST. HAPPY BIRTHDAY is $675 plus HST. You get the written quote first, and nothing is booked until you say yes.
